# 应用描述

## 中文

让相册里的风景，成为可以回看的故事。

AI旅行日记帮你整理旅行照片，用图文留住回忆。

- 照片游记：选择至少3张照片和喜欢的风格，由AI生成可编辑的游记。
- 萌宠陪伴：和小橘聊聊日常与旅行，通过记忆锚点回看记录。
- 回忆整理：按月份浏览日记，探索成长记录与个人洞察。
- 素材创作：管理照片、生成AI配图，搭配贴纸和已解锁城市印章制作明信片。
- 自主分享：日记默认私密，由你选择公开段落；明信片可保存或通过系统分享。

AI内容提供来源标识，可能存在偏差，请在保存或分享前核对。AI功能需要联网，相关照片和文字由服务器及第三方AI服务处理，详见隐私政策。萌宠陪伴和洞察仅用于日常交流与自我回顾，不提供医疗或心理治疗。

## English

Turn the places in your camera roll into stories you can revisit.

AI Travel Diary helps you keep travel memories in words and photos.

- Photo journals: Choose at least 3 photos and a style to create an editable AI-assisted travel journal.
- AI companion: Chat with Xiaoju about everyday life and travel, and revisit records through memory anchors.
- Memories: Browse journals by month and explore growth records and personal insights.
- Creative materials: Manage photos, generate AI images, and make postcards with stickers and unlocked city stamps.
- Sharing controls: Journals are private by default. Choose the sections to publish, or save and share postcards through the system share sheet.

AI content includes origin notices and may contain inaccuracies. Review it before saving or sharing. AI features require internet access; relevant photos and text are processed by backend and third-party AI services. See the privacy policy. Chat and insights are for everyday conversation and reflection, not medical or psychological treatment.
